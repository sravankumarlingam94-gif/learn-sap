Practice lab

A teaching model of the SAP transaction flow, built around the entries an accounts and stores desk actually makes: requisitions and purchase orders, goods receipt, stores reservations and draw against a cost centre, contractor service entry, vendor bills with and without a purchase order, outgoing payment, the payroll posting, and journal entries — plus gate entry and weighment, quality inspection with an accept or reject usage decision, inbound freight booking, and the full asset cycle from master to depreciation run. For IT teams there is a support desk too: users and roles, authorization checks, background jobs, runtime errors, the data browser and transports from development to production. Type a transaction code or pick one below, fill the screen, and save. Documents carry forward, stock moves, cost centres get charged, and the system pushes back when the data is wrong. Nothing here connects to SAP software; it is a simulator for learning the logic.

ABAP workbench

A working interpreter for a teaching subset of ABAP. Declarations, arithmetic, conditions, DO loops and LOOP AT over the tables all run. The tables hold the documents you created in the lab, so a report written here reads your own purchase orders.

SE38 · report source
List output
Execute a report to see its list.

Supported: DATA lv TYPE i VALUE 0. lv = lv + 1. WRITE: / 'text', lv. IF a > b. ELSE. ENDIF. DO 5 TIMES. ENDDO. LOOP AT mara INTO ls. ENDLOOP. ULINE. SKIP. Tables available: mara (matnr, maktx, stock, price), ekko (ebeln, lifnr, value), vbak (vbeln, kunnr, value), qals (prueflos, matnr, losmenge, accepted, rejected, status), anla (anln1, txt50, value, dep). Operators need spaces around them, as in real ABAP.

Nine modules, one system

Each module is a department of the same company, working on the same data. Learn to recognise them by their transaction codes, menus and tables — that is how people on a project tell at a glance whose problem a ticket is.

The four stages of an SAP career

Pay rises when responsibility rises. Each stage is defined by what you are trusted to decide, not by how long you have been sitting there.

Figures are indicative market ranges for India in 2026, modelled from published module-wise salary bands and adjusted for employer type, city market index and proven supporting skills. They describe what the market pays on average, not what any employer will offer you.

Actual compensation depends on interview performance, the specific project, notice period, negotiation and timing. Treat the numbers as a planning tool for deciding what to learn next.
